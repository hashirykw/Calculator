Nexlyr Calculator: about and help

Nexlyr Calculator is a free scientific calculator, step-by-step solver and graphing calculator for students, with an AI maths tutor called Amy. It works in any modern browser, installs to your home screen and keeps working offline (everything except Amy and live currency rates). It is made by Nexlyr Solutions in Pakistan.

Scientific calculator

Type whole expressions with the correct order of operations (2 + 3 × 4 = 14), see a live preview, and switch between exact and decimal answers with S⇔D: 1/3, √8 = 2√2 and sin 60° = √3/2 are shown exactly. Angles in degrees, radians or grads; memory keys; Ans; factorial, combinations and permutations; percentages that work like a desk calculator; complex numbers when you switch them on.

Solve with real working

Solve linear, quadratic and higher equations, simultaneous equations and inequalities. Differentiate with the product, quotient and chain rules shown step by step, integrate with substitution, integration by parts and partial fractions, find limits with L’Hôpital’s rule, factorise and expand, and work out Taylor series, prime factors, sums, matrices and probabilities. You can type plain English such as “solve 2x + 3 = 11”, “derivative of x^3 sin x” or “integrate x^2 from 0 to 3”. Every answer is checked numerically before it is shown; if a check fails, no answer is shown.

Graphing studio

Plot up to ten functions, relations such as x² + y² = 9, polar curves, parametric curves, inequalities, points and vector fields. Letters such as a and k become sliders you can animate. Trace curves, mark roots, turning points and intersections, show the derivative, shade the area under a curve, read a table of values, export a PNG or share a link. Switch to 3D to draw surfaces z = f(x, y).

Amy, the AI maths tutor

Ask Amy any maths or science question in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu, or send a photo of a question. Amy explains the method step by step, and can explain any answer from the calculator. Amy is powered by Anthropic’s Claude.

Tools

  • Unit converter with 16 categories, including currency (PKR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR and more).
  • Matrix calculator from 1×1 to 6×6: determinant, inverse, rank, RREF, eigenvalues and solving Ax = b.
  • Statistics: mean, median, mode, quartiles, standard deviation, box plots, histograms, regression, normal, binomial and Poisson distributions.
  • Trigonometry: unit circle, exact values and a triangle solver for SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS and the ambiguous SSA case.
  • Formula library for O Level, IGCSE and A Level maths, physics and chemistry, with solve-for-any-variable.
  • Number bases 2–36, two’s complement, bitwise operations and text to code points.
  • Money: loan and EMI with full amortisation, savings and compound interest, percentages, discounts, profit and loss, tip split.
  • Student tools: equation solver forms, fractions, percentage and grade, GPA/CGPA, zakat, date and age, time, SUVAT and physics, molar mass and chemistry, and an exam mode.

Privacy

Your history and settings stay on your device. Messages to Amy are sent to Anthropic’s Claude to generate answers and are not stored on our server.
